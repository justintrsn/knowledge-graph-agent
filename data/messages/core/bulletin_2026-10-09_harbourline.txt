From: Technical Dept, Harbourline Ship Management
To: Masters and Chief Engineers, Harbourline fleet
Date: 2026-10-09 10:00 UTC
Subject: Fleet bulletin TB-26-14: seawater cooling pump seals, Meridian 8500 class

Following repeated seawater cooling pump mechanical seal failures on Meridian 8500 class vessels this year (Ocean Star, Ocean Pearl),
all Meridian 8500 vessels under our management must carry two spare seal kits and inspect seals at every port call.
Root-cause investigation with the pump maker is ongoing. Not yet confirmed whether the Kestrel 4000 class pumps are affected.
